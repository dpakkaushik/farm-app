## The architecture decisions (do not relitigate)
- A bill is the debit; payment is against the PARTY, never a bill.
- Founding figures (all opening balances, opening_cost, capex_threshold,
  go_live_date, opening_cash) are **admin-only + audit-logged**, enforced by DB
  trigger `guard_founding_figures()` — UI checks are cosmetic, the anon key ships
  in the bundle.
- **Accounts**: every rupee names its pocket. receipt +1 / payment −1 / transfer
  ±pair (nets to zero, never P&L). Payment **mode routes the account**
  (`accountFor(mode)` in store — regex match incl. 'bank_transfer'); mills default
  bank, local crop sales default cash. `owner_drawing` entry type exists — never
  an expense.
- Capital: below capex_threshold → expensed that month; at/above → capital,
  OUT of P&L, shown via v_capital_purchases + Summary's Capital Purchases card.
  NO depreciation (user declined). Dairy animals capital; broiler batches inventory.
- Every rupee lives in exactly ONE place: an opening figure OR a transaction.
- SaaS rule: every new master must decide its day-one story — seeded default
  (`seed_farm_defaults` / `seed_farm_accounts`), empty-until-added, or app constant.
