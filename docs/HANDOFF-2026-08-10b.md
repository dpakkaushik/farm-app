## What was fixed, and why it mattered
**Every native `<select>` in the app had unreadable options.** Selects carry a
translucent background (`--c-ghost`), which makes the browser abandon its native dark
popup and paint option rows near-white — while option text inherits white. White on
white, visible only under the hover highlight. Fixed once in `index.css` with an opaque
`select option` rule, so all dropdowns in both themes are fixed, not just the reported one.

**Pay Vendor still offered four payment modes** (cash / bank_transfer / cheque / upi)
from before the accounts work. Now a two-button toggle — 💵 Cash | 🏦 Bank Transfer —
matching the sale forms. **The stored value is still `bank_transfer`**, because
`vendor_payments_payment_mode_check` predates 0028 and only accepts the old four
strings; `accountFor()` routes it to the Bank pocket either way. Do not "clean this up"
to `'bank'` without also altering the CHECK constraint.

Also: vendor khata rows no longer label every payment "Cash Payment".

## The financial-statements decision (2026-08-10) — do not relitigate
The owner's director asked for **Cash Flow, Trial Balance, Balance Sheet**. Assessed and
agreed with the owner; full reasoning is in the memory file `financial-statements-decision.md`.

- **Cash Flow — YES, build it.** Use the **direct** method: classify actual cash
  movements into operating / investing / financing. The indirect method needs opening
  and closing balance sheets, which don't exist. Every `owner_cash_entries` row already
  carries `entry_type` (`revenue_receipt`, `vendor_payment`, `labour_payment`,
  `expense_payment`, `owner_capital`, `owner_drawing`, `transfer`, `opening_cash`), so
  this is largely regrouping. **Known approximation:** a payment settles the *party, not
  the bill*, so a lump payment to a vendor who sold both seed and a sprayer cannot be
  split cleanly into operating vs investing. Say so in the UI rather than faking precision.

- **Trial Balance — NO. Do not build it.** It is a *proof* that debits equal credits.
  This app is not double-entry; it is purpose-built views over transaction tables. A
  screen listing balances would look like a trial balance and prove nothing — worse than
  absent, because it invites trust it hasn't earned. Build the director's actual intent
  instead as a **Books Health check**: cash book vs account balances, purchases with no
  party attached (~₹78k today), bill header vs lines, every rupee exactly once. This
  check — not the Balance Sheet — is the piece that must not be skipped.

- **Balance Sheet — DEFERRED,** blocked on three numbers only the owner/director has:
  **land + plot value**, **loans against the farm**, **what counts as owner capital**.
  No depreciation by the owner's choice, so assets sit at cost forever. Pieces that do
  exist: party khata (payables), buyer khata (receivables), the two accounts (cash),
  `v_capital_purchases` (assets), inventory.

### Sequencing correction that matters
Cash Flow is **not** blocked by the full go-live pass — it needs only the **opening cash
and bank** figures, because misattributed crop costs are a P&L problem, not a cash one.
The rupee left the account on the day it left, whichever cycle gets blamed. The
**Balance Sheet is** blocked by the go-live pass.
